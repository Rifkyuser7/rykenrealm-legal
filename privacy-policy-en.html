Privacy Policy

This policy explains how RykenRealm handles information and data when users interact with the Discord bot.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

01. Introduction

RykenRealm respects user privacy. This policy explains the types of information that may be processed, how information is used, and matters related to data storage and protection when using the RykenRealm Discord bot.

02. Information We Process

RykenRealm may process information necessary to operate its features, including:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Game data and user progression.
  • Virtual economy and inventory data.
  • Virtual partner, family, and related interaction data.
  • Settings and internal data required for bot operation.

03. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate features, maintain progression, preserve data consistency, address misuse, and improve the stability and functionality of RykenRealm.

Information is not intended to be used for personal activity tracking outside the operational needs of the bot.

04. Discord Messages and Interactions

RykenRealm may read certain message content required by active features, such as XP systems, birthday celebrations, or game interactions.

RykenRealm is not designed to store users' entire conversation history as a chat archive.

05. Data Storage

Bot data is stored using database systems and storage mechanisms intended to maintain user data and the state of bot features.

Storage may involve third-party services required to operate RykenRealm's infrastructure.

06. Third-Party Services

Some RykenRealm features may use external services to process specific requests.

  • Discord — bot platform and user interactions.
  • MongoDB — data storage.
  • Jikan — retrieval of certain anime information.
  • Frankfurter — exchange rate data.
  • CoinGecko — cryptocurrency information.
  • Vyro — image processing for certain features.

External services have their own privacy policies and data handling practices, which are outside RykenRealm's direct control.

07. Data Protection

Reasonable efforts are made to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse. However, no digital storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08. Data Retention

Data may be retained for as long as necessary to support features, maintain system integrity, and meet operational requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data and system conditions.

09. Data Deletion

Users who wish to request the deletion of data associated with their account may contact the RykenRealm administrator through the available official contact channels.

Requests will be reviewed based on the administrator's capabilities and the condition of the stored data.

10. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in features, systems, services, or operational requirements. Updates will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding privacy and data, please contact the RykenRealm administrator through the official community channels or Discord.
