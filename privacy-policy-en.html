Privacy Policy

Information about the data processed by RykenRealm and how it is handled.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to RykenRealm. This Privacy Policy explains how the RykenRealm Discord bot collects, processes, uses, and stores information when users interact with our services.

By using RykenRealm, you acknowledge that certain information may be necessary for the bot's features to function.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, RykenRealm may process the following information:

  • Discord information: User ID, username or display name, and server information required for bot functionality.
  • Economy data: Virtual balances, transactions, jobs, assets, stocks, and in-bot economic activity.
  • Game data: XP, levels, RPG statistics, inventory, missions, game progress, and related information.
  • Social data: Virtual partner, family, virtual children, and related settings.
  • Preferences: Language, notifications, customization, and available feature preferences.
  • Donation and support records: Records of donations or support status needed to manage rewards.
  • Activity data: Cooldowns, feature activity records, and other information required to prevent abuse.

3. Discord Message Processing

RykenRealm may process Discord message content in certain situations to support bot functionality.

  • Guild messages may be processed for the XP system and certain activity features.
  • Messages may be checked to trigger birthday celebrations for registered birthdays.
  • Direct messages (DMs) may be processed as input for certain games, such as Third Seven Eleven.

RykenRealm is not designed to store all user conversations as chat history. However, temporary message processing may occur when relevant features are used.

4. How We Use Information

Processed information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate RykenRealm features.
  • Maintain account progress, virtual economy data, and game progress.
  • Manage XP, levels, cooldowns, and rewards.
  • Operate social and personalization features.
  • Prevent abuse, manipulation, and rule violations.
  • Maintain system stability and improve service quality.
  • Handle user support and donation records.

5. Data Storage

Service data is stored using database infrastructure, including MongoDB. The system also has local storage fallback mechanisms in certain configurations.

Data is retained for as long as necessary to provide features, maintain system integrity, support operations, or address abuse and security issues.

Access to data is restricted to authorized parties for service operation and management purposes.

6. Third-Party Services

Some RykenRealm features rely on external services to provide specific functionality. Relevant information may be sent to these services when those features are used.

  • Discord: The platform where the bot operates and interacts with users.
  • MongoDB: Database infrastructure used to store service data.
  • Jikan API: Used to retrieve anime character information.
  • Frankfurter API: Used to retrieve currency exchange rate information.
  • CoinGecko API: Used to retrieve cryptocurrency market price information.
  • Vyro Inference Engine: Used by the image upscaling feature. Images selected by users are sent to this external service for processing.

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy and terms. RykenRealm does not control all data-handling practices of external providers.

For image upscaling, we cannot confirm the external provider's image retention or deletion practices. Avoid uploading sensitive or confidential images.

7. Files and Media

Certain features allow users to upload attachments for processing, including media conversion and image processing.

Media conversion uses the processing mechanisms available within the bot. Image upscaling uses an external service as described above.

Do not submit files containing private, confidential, or unauthorized material.

8. Information Security

We aim to apply reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse.

However, no data storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Deletion

If you wish to request deletion of data associated with your Discord account, please contact the RykenRealm administrators through an official contact channel.

Requests may require account ownership verification to prevent unauthorized deletion. Certain information may need to be retained for security, system integrity, or applicable legal requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

RykenRealm is a Discord bot, and its use is subject to Discord's age requirements and applicable laws in each user's region.

We do not intend to knowingly collect children's personal information beyond what is necessary for service operation.

11. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to features, systems, services, or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be published through the RykenRealm Legal Center.

12. Contact

If you have questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the RykenRealm administrators through our Discord server or an official project contact channel.

By using RykenRealm, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
