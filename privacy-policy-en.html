Privacy Policy — RykenRealm

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected, stored, and processed by the Discord bot RykenRealm (hereinafter referred to as "RykenRealm" or the "Bot"), developed and managed by Rizuki (@ryin.r). By using the Bot, you acknowledge the practices described below.

Official community server: https://discord.gg/kME469thr6

1. Data Collected and Stored

The Bot may collect and store the following information to operate its features and maintain user progress:

  • Discord Account Information — Discord User IDs and display names used to identify users and associate their data with their accounts.
  • Server Information — Discord Guild IDs used for server activation, whitelist management, and server-specific features such as leaderboards.
  • Virtual Economy Data — Virtual currency balances, job progress, virtual property ownership, virtual stocks and cryptocurrency records, and other economy-related progress.
  • RPG Data — Character levels, inventories, dungeon and tower progress, crafting information, and other RPG-related data.
  • Fictional Partner and Family Data — Anime character selections, fictional relationship information, and fictional child data associated with the Bot's partner and family features.
  • User Preferences — Language settings and other preferences provided or selected when using supported Bot features.
  • Usage and Cooldown Information — Feature usage records, cooldown timestamps, and relevant activity statistics used to maintain feature functionality and help prevent abuse.

The Bot does not intend to store the contents of ordinary, non-command conversations as part of its user database. However, certain features may process message activity temporarily when required for their functionality, such as chat activity or experience systems.

2. Data Processed by Third-Party Services

  • AI Chat and Image Analysis — Messages, prompts, or images submitted through supported AI features may be forwarded to Google Gemini API for processing and generating responses. Data handling by Google is governed by the Google Privacy Policy . The Bot does not intend to permanently store AI conversation history in its own user database.
  • Media Conversion — Files submitted through supported attachment-based media conversion features may be temporarily processed to generate the requested output. Processing depends on the feature and file type. Uploaded files are not intended to be retained permanently by the Bot after processing.
  • Anime Character Search — Character names submitted through the partner feature may be sent to Jikan API to retrieve publicly available anime character information from MyAnimeList.

3. How Data Is Used

Information collected by the Bot is used for purposes such as:

  • Operating Bot features and maintaining user progress across sessions.
  • Displaying leaderboards, rankings, and statistics within supported Discord servers.
  • Managing user preferences and feature settings.
  • Enforcing cooldowns and helping prevent abuse, exploitation, or manipulation of Bot systems.

User data is not sold or rented to third parties. The Bot does not intentionally share user data with third parties for commercial advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • User data is stored using the database and hosting infrastructure configured for the Bot's operation.
  • Access to the Bot's hosting environment and database is restricted to authorized management access.
  • Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information. However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.
  • The Bot does not request Discord passwords, payment card details, or government-issued identity documents as part of its normal features.

5. Third-Party Services

The Bot may interact with the following third-party services to provide its features:

Service Purpose
Discord API Discord platform integration and Bot functionality.
Google Gemini API AI chat and image analysis features.
Jikan API (MyAnimeList) Retrieving public anime character information.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Their data handling practices are outside the direct control of the Bot owner.

6. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users may:

  • Request Data Deletion — Contact Rizuki (@ryin.r) through the official community server to request the deletion of data associated with their Bot account. Requests may require verification to ensure that the data belongs to the requesting user.
  • Stop Using the Bot — Users may stop interacting with the Bot at any time or ask a Discord server administrator to remove it from their server.

Removing the Bot from a server or stopping its use does not necessarily delete previously stored user data. A separate deletion request may be required.

7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is subject to the minimum age requirements established by the Discord Terms of Service and applicable local requirements. The Bot is not intended to be used by individuals who are below the minimum permitted age.

8. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary, including when Bot features, data practices, or service infrastructure change. The latest version will be published on the official Legal Center. Significant changes may also be announced through the official community server.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, data-related requests, or concerns regarding this policy, contact:
